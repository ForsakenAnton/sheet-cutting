Sheet Cutting Planner

Free online cut list optimizer for sheet materials

Enter a sheet size and a list of parts — plywood, MDF, chipboard, glass, metal — and get an optimized cutting layout: blade kerf and edge margin, 90° rotation, several sheets when the parts do not fit on one, a cut list with utilization and waste.

Export the plan to PDF or SVG, or print it. Millimetres, centimetres, metres or inches. Free, works in your browser, no sign-up: the project stays on your device.
